  Project weight: +15 High = BDA M.S., DiCE Lab, Caltrans, GEOG 582, COMPE 510, BDA 696, SCIBER-CT,

      +0 Low = FAMAIL, Construction Diagram/Doc AI.
  Mentions: +5 per mention beyond the first, capped at +15.
  Owed to a person or blocking someone (Context or Waiting On names them): +10.
  Effort × proximity: XS/S due within 3 days +5 (quick win) · L due within 7 days +10 (start now).
  Staleness: Inbox/Next with no Due and Created more than 14 days ago: +5.
  Manual Priority: P0 floor 85 · P1 floor 65 · P3 cap 40. A hard deadline within 2 days may

  Clamp to 0–100. Priority: ≥80 P0 · ≥60 P1 · ≥35 P2 · else P3.
